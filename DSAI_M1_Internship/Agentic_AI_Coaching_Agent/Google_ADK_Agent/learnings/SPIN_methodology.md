# SPIN Selling — Quick Reference

## The Framework

| Phase | What | Goal | Time |
|-------|------|------|------|
| **S**ituation | Factual questions about current state | Establish context | 2-3 min |
| **P**roblem | Questions about difficulties/pain | Surface needs | 5-7 min |
| **I**mplication | Questions about impact/consequences | Build urgency | 5-8 min |
| **N**eed-Payoff | Questions about value of solving | Customer sells themselves | 3-5 min |

## Key Insight

Average reps spend too long on Situation and rush to present.
Top performers spend most time on Problem and Implication — that's where deals are won.

## The Rule

> Never present your solution until the customer has said (in their own words)
> why they need it.

## Source

Based on Neil Rackham's research (35,000+ sales calls analyzed).
Book: "SPIN Selling" (1988).
